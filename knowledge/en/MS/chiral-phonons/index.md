# Chiral Phonons

Phonons carrying intrinsic angular momentum represent a frontier in condensed matter physics. This series covers the theory, materials, experimental detection, and applications of chiral phonons—from valley-phonon coupling in 2D materials to phonon angular momentum transport.

**Advanced Topic** | 4 Chapters | Approximately 8 hours

---

## Series Overview

Chiral phonons are lattice vibrations that carry angular momentum, discovered in 2015 in monolayer WSe₂. Unlike conventional phonons described by scalar displacement fields, chiral phonons exhibit circular atomic motion and couple to valley degrees of freedom in 2D materials. This emerging field connects phonon physics with topology, valleytronics, and quantum information science.

### What You'll Learn

- Phonon angular momentum (PAM) and its quantization
- Symmetry requirements for chiral phonons
- Valley-phonon coupling in TMD monolayers
- Experimental techniques: circularly polarized Raman spectroscopy
- Computational methods for calculating phonon chirality
- Applications in valleytronics and phonon-based devices

### Prerequisites

- Understanding of phonon dispersion and lattice dynamics
- Basic group theory and symmetry operations
- Familiarity with 2D materials (helpful but not required)
- Quantum mechanics fundamentals
