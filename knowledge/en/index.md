## Comprehensive Learning Platform for Data-Driven R&D

We provide systematic educational content covering six domains: Materials Informatics (MI), Process Informatics (PI), Machine Learning (ML), Materials Science Fundamentals (MS), Fundamentals of Mathematics & Physics (FM), and Quantum Computing (QC). All content is available in Japanese with abundant code examples to acquire practical skills from fundamentals to real-world applications.
