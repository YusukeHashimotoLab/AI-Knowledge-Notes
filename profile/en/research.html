Autonomous Experiment System

Robotic Arm × Electric Pipette for Automated Materials Synthesis

We built a two-solution mixing automated experiment system consisting of an electric pipette, a robotic arm, a temperature-controlled magnetic stirrer, an electronic balance, and a camera, and use it to study how operating conditions such as injection speed affect the synthesis of ZIF-8, a representative metal-organic framework (MOF). The aim is "nanomaterial synthesis process informatics": accumulating operation data that the literature does not usually record, paired with the resulting material properties.

  • Challenge: Operating parameters such as injection speed influence synthesis outcomes, yet they are rarely recorded in papers and are not accumulated as data
  • Approach: The electric pipette controls injection volume and injection speed in nine steps, and the injected mass is recorded by the balance; volume, speed, temperature, mass and robot motions are all logged as metadata
  • Confirmed results: A 2-methylimidazole/TEA solution was injected into a Zn(NO3)2/PEG solution in DMF over 5 s, 2 s and 0.5 s. XRD confirmed ZIF-8 under all conditions, and DLS showed that slower injection yields larger particles, which we interpret as a shift in the balance between nucleation and growth with injection speed
  • Next steps: Link the accumulated operation data to material properties for machine-learning prediction and process optimization

Multi-Agent AI for Autonomous Data Analysis and Knowledge-Base Construction

LLM Agents × Materials Experimental Databases

Targeting Starrydata2, the NIMS experimental database of thermoelectric materials (about 209,000 data points from about 14,000 papers), we are developing a system in which multiple AI agents cooperate to run the cycle from data analysis to knowledge-base construction autonomously and repeatedly.

  • Challenge: Exhaustive manual analysis of hundreds of thousands of experimental data points is impractical, and a large language model on its own is not good at reading trends directly from numerical data
  • Approach: A three-layer, nine-agent multi-agent AI system implemented on Claude Code, whose agents share data extraction, feature generation, machine-learning modeling, SHAP analysis, discussion, knowledge-base construction, and report writing and review, with safeguards against data leakage, anomalously high R², overfitting and physical-constraint violations
  • Confirmed results: 417 projects over 100 topics produced about 8,110 knowledge entries. Experiments varying the number of topics and cycles from 1×1 to 10×10 showed that knowledge inherited across cycles expands exploration to related materials (Bi2Te3 → Bi2Se3) and deepens it
  • Application: The resulting knowledge base is used to build a specialized AI (GPTs) for thermoelectric materials

Ultrafast Time-Resolved Magneto-Optical Spectroscopy

TRMOI & Spin Wave Tomography

Independently developed an ultimate video camera (TRMOI) with 100-femtosecond temporal resolution and 1-micrometer spatial resolution. Based on this technology, established the Spin Wave Tomography (SWaT) method to reconstruct dispersion relations of magnetic excitation propagation processes from experimental data.

  • 100-femtosecond temporal and 1-micrometer spatial resolution achieved simultaneously (Rev. Sci. Instrum. 85, 063702, 2014)
  • All-optical reconstruction of spin wave dispersion relations
  • Innovation in magneto-optical imaging technology
  • Elucidation of anomalous phenomena in spin Seebeck effects

Major Achievements

  • Published in Nature Communications (83 citations)
  • Published in Review of Scientific Instruments
  • Patent obtained for magneto-optical measurement apparatus
  • Core technology in JST ERATO project

High-Repetition-Rate Ultrashort Pulse Semiconductor Laser Spectroscopy

Development of Simplified Magnetic Spectroscopy

Conceived and demonstrated an innovative magnetic spectroscopy method using high-repetition-rate ultrashort pulse semiconductor lasers and repetitive synchronization phenomena of magneto-optical excitation processes, significantly simplifying conventional complex measurement systems.

  • Achieved significant simplification of measurement systems
  • Principle verification through simulation
  • Applications to spintronic device evaluation
  • Design oriented toward industrial practical implementation

Intellectual Property
