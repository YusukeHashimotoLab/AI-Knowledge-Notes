Materials Map

A graph-based machine-learning map that integrates the experimental database Starrydata2 with the computational database Materials Project, used to find analogues of promising thermoelectric materials.

Y. Hashimoto, X. Jia, H. Li, T. Tomai, APL Machine Learning 3, 036104 (2025)

Automated MOF Synthesis

A robotic arm and an electric pipette control the injection speed of the precursor solution. For ZIF-8, slower injection tended to give larger particles under the tested conditions; injection volume, speed, temperature and other process data are logged as metadata for process informatics.

Oral presentation, SCEJ 57th Autumn Meeting (September 14, 2026)

Multi-Agent AI for Autonomous Data Analysis

A three-layer, nine-agent system implemented on Claude Code analyses the thermoelectric database Starrydata2 autonomously. 417 projects produced about 8,110 knowledge entries, and inheriting knowledge across cycles expands and deepens the exploration.

Society of Data-driven Materials Science 2026 Annual Meeting (July 6, 2026); poster at the Agentic AI Summit 2026, UC Berkeley (August 1–2, 2026); 87th JSAP Autumn Meeting (September 10, 2026)
